FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Find answers to the most common questions about our laundry services, pickup and delivery, pricing and more.

What laundry services do you offer? +

We offer Service Wash, Ironing, Dry Cleaning, Houseware cleaning and Bedlinen care. We also provide pickup and delivery services for added convenience.

How much does your laundry service cost? +

Our prices depend on the service and item you choose. You can view our available prices on the Pricing section of our website.

How long does the laundry service take? +

Turnaround time can vary depending on the service and the amount of laundry. Our team will provide an estimated completion time when you arrange your service.

Do you provide business laundry services? +

Yes. We provide laundry solutions for businesses including hotels, B&Bs, restaurants, Airbnb and rental properties, salons, gyms and offices. Visit our B2B page to learn more.

Can I request a specific laundry service? +

Yes. You can choose the service that best suits your needs. If you're unsure which service is appropriate for an item, contact us and we'll be happy to advise you.

Do you offer pickup and delivery? +

Yes. We can collect your laundry and return it to you once your selected service has been completed. Contact us to arrange a pickup.

How do I book a pickup? +

Click the "Book a Pickup" button on our website and follow the booking instructions. You can also contact our team directly if you need assistance.

Do you clean delicate clothing? +

Yes. We offer specialist cleaning services for delicate garments and items that require extra care. Please let us know about any special instructions when booking.

What areas do you serve? +

We proudly serve Sydney, Sydney River, North Sydney, Glace Bay, Sydney Mines, New Waterford, Dominion, Reserve Mines and surrounding areas.

How can I contact KES Laundry? +

You can contact us through the Contact section of our website. Our team will be happy to answer your questions and help you arrange your laundry service.
